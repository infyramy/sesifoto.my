# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Malaysian photography studio owners looking to manage bookings, payments, and client communication. Their customers are families and individuals booking Raya (Eid) photoshoots. Studio photographers who need assignment schedules.

## Product Purpose
SesiFoto is a SaaS booking and studio management platform for Malaysian photography studios. It automates booking forms, calendar syncing, WhatsApp notifications, payment processing, and photographer scheduling. Success looks like studios running without manual booking coordination.

## Brand Personality
Professional, warm, confident. A reliable business tool that speaks the creative studio language. Not corporate or cold; approachable but capable.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS landing pages with blue gradients and stock photos
- Overly playful/childish UI that undermines professionalism
- Cluttered dashboards with too many metrics visible at once

## Design Principles
1. **Show the product, not the promise** — real UI mockups, real data patterns, not abstract illustrations
2. **Warm professionalism** — orange/amber palette grounded by dark neutrals, never neon
3. **Speed is trust** — a fast-loading page signals a fast product
4. **Bilingual fluency** — BM and EN must feel equally native, not translated
5. **Mobile-first reality** — Malaysian studios browse on phones first

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion support already implemented. Dark mode forced by default. Bilingual (Malay/English) with instant language switching.
